By using this app, you consent to the collection of data by Google for advertising purposes, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD).

3. Target Audience and Children’s Privacy

The target audience for this game is individuals aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, you should not use this application.

4. Data Security

While we do not store your data on our own servers, we encourage users to review the privacy settings of their Google account and mobile device to manage how their advertising ID is shared.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the application Chicken Foot Board, developed by Dione R. Rocha.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Chicken Foot Board does not collect, store, track, transmit, or share any personal, sensitive, or identifiable user data.

  • No User Accounts: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to play or use the application.
  • Local Data Only: All game settings, progress, scores, and preferences are saved strictly locally on your device. Clearing your app data or uninstalling the app will reset your local saved data.
  • No Ads or In-App Purchases: The application contains no advertisements, no tracking SDKs, no advertising IDs (AAID), and no in-app billing services.

2. Device Permissions

The application requires only basic system capabilities. It does not access your camera, contacts, microphone, device location, storage files, or device advertising identifier.

3. Children's Privacy

Because Chicken Foot Board does not collect any personal data or use tracking/advertising identifiers, it is safe for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

4. Third-Party Services

This application does not integrate third-party analytics services, advertising networks (such as Google AdMob), or payment processors. No data is shared with external third-party companies.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
